Welcome in. This is where I document labs, walkthroughs, and the occasional production war story.

## How these are written

Everything here starts life as a note in **Obsidian** while I'm working through a lab. When it's polished enough to share, the markdown file gets dropped into this site's content folder and published — no CMS, no copy-paste reformatting.

## What to expect

- **Lab walkthroughs** — full topologies with configs, verification output, and the mistakes I made along the way.
- **Deep dives** — one protocol or feature, taken apart properly.
- **Field notes** — shorter posts on things that bit me in the real world.

Each post carries a difficulty rating, from *Beginner* to *Expert*. Ratings are about how much background you need, not how clever the post is.

The log is short for now. That's the point — watch this space.
